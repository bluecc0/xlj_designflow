# Theme Map

Use themes to infer mood, symbols, audience, and visual taboos. Do not treat this as a fixed lookup table; use the same reasoning for unseen themes.

## Children's Day / 儿童节

- Mood: cute, warm, cheerful, bright, playful, safe.
- Audience: children, parents, school, education, family activities, commercial campaigns.
- Lettering: rounded, soft, inflated, sticker-like, handwritten, crayon, marker, candy, paper-cut.
- Colors: pastel yellow, sky blue, soft pink, mint green, warm white.
- Symbols: balloons, rainbow, stars, clouds, toys, blocks, ribbons, smiling shapes.
- Avoid: dark horror, sharp aggression, cold metal, adult sensuality, oppressive contrast.

## Spring Festival / 春节

- Mood: festive, abundant, warm, energetic, family-oriented.
- Lettering: full, thick, auspicious, paper-cut, brush, folk poster, gold embossing if restrained.
- Colors: red, vermilion, warm gold, ivory, black ink accents.
- Symbols: lanterns, couplets, firecrackers, clouds, flowers, paper-cut patterns.
- Avoid: cheap red-gold clutter, random zodiac overuse, illegible brush chaos.

## Dragon Boat Festival / 端午

- Mood: traditional, fresh, seasonal, clean, folk.
- Lettering: leaf-wrapped strokes, brush structure, woven cord accents, water movement.
- Colors: mugwort green, rice white, dark teal, cinnabar accent.
- Symbols: zongzi leaves, dragon boat, water waves, mugwort, five-color thread.
- Avoid: making it look like Spring Festival, excessive red-gold, childish cartoon unless requested.

## Mid-Autumn Festival / 中秋

- Mood: reunion, moonlight, elegance, quiet warmth.
- Lettering: soft brush, moon-shaped curves, jade or paper texture, restrained decorative rhythm.
- Colors: moon white, warm gold, deep blue, osmanthus yellow, muted green.
- Symbols: moon, osmanthus, clouds, jade rabbit if appropriate, window lattice.
- Avoid: too many lanterns, noisy festival clutter, overdone fantasy glow.

## Valentine's / 七夕 / 情人节

- Mood: romantic, soft, intimate, elegant, sweet.
- Lettering: flowing, ribbon-like, glossy, handwritten, floral, light serif-like contrast.
- Colors: rose, blush, wine red, pearl, champagne, soft violet.
- Symbols: ribbons, flowers, stars, heart motifs used sparingly.
- Avoid: tacky heart overload, cheap neon pink, illegible cursive.

## National Day / 国庆

- Mood: grand, bright, ceremonial, energetic.
- Lettering: bold, upright, high-impact, banner-like, monumental.
- Colors: red, gold, white, sky blue accents.
- Symbols: flags, ribbons, rays, city silhouette, celebratory confetti.
- Avoid: excessive political poster stiffness unless requested, tiny unreadable detail.

## School Season / 开学季

- Mood: fresh, energetic, optimistic, youthful.
- Lettering: clean handwritten, notebook, sticker, campus poster, chalk, marker.
- Colors: sky blue, green, yellow, white, light orange.
- Symbols: books, pencils, paper planes, campus, stars.
- Avoid: infantile style if targeting college students.

## Graduation / 毕业季

- Mood: nostalgic, hopeful, youthful, ceremonial.
- Lettering: handwritten, yearbook, ribbon, sunlight, campus poster, clean display lettering.
- Colors: blue, white, warm yellow, grass green, muted gold.
- Symbols: graduation cap, paper plane, photos, campus trees, sunlight.
- Avoid: childish school-season look if tone is mature.

## Shopping Campaign / 促销 / 周年庆

- Mood: high-energy, commercial, direct, festive.
- Lettering: bold, thick, high-contrast, readable, central.
- Colors: depends on brand; common options are red, yellow, black, white, brand colors.
- Symbols: ribbons, price-tag shapes, bursts, stage lights, confetti.
- Avoid: clutter that hides the exact text; too many effects; cheap bevels.

## Unknown Theme Inference

For a theme not listed:

1. Identify literal meaning.
2. Identify likely audience.
3. Identify event category: festival, campaign, product, culture, entertainment, personal expression.
4. Choose 2-4 relevant symbols.
5. Choose mood and taboo directions.
6. Convert into stroke, material, decoration, and composition.
