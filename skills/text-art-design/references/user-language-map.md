# User Language Map

Translate casual user phrases into design constraints.

## Common Phrases

可爱一点:
- rounded strokes, soft terminals, pastel colors, sticker/candy/plush material, low aggression.

温馨一点:
- warm light, handwritten or rounded structure, soft contrast, family-friendly symbols, less hard geometry.

高级一点:
- fewer colors, more negative space, restrained material, cleaner silhouette, less decoration, consistent lighting.

别太土:
- reduce red-gold clutter, avoid cheap bevels, avoid default clipart, reduce heavy outlines, use fewer elements.

更炸裂:
- thicker title mass, stronger contrast, dynamic strokes, dramatic light, bigger silhouette, controlled effects.

更有冲击力:
- large centered title, bold readable structure, unified outer contour, high contrast, simplified background.

像 logo:
- no busy background, strong silhouette, fewer materials, front-facing composition, scalable shape, clear outline.

像海报标题:
- allow mood background, cinematic lighting, strong title hierarchy, more atmosphere than logo.

像小朋友画的:
- crayon, marker, colored pencil, irregular hand-drawn edges, playful rhythm, but still accurate characters.

更商业:
- cleaner title, higher readability, stronger product/campaign hierarchy, fewer experimental distortions.

更国风:
- Chinese cultural symbols, ink/paper/jade/lacquer/cloud/wave motifs, but avoid random ancient props.

更年轻:
- brighter palette, sticker/neon/retro/pop rhythm, bolder shape, less formal calligraphy.

不要那么 AI:
- reduce overglow, reduce random particles, simplify background, use coherent material, avoid meaningless symbols.

字不清楚:
- preserve original Chinese structure, reduce decoration invading strokes, increase contrast, front-facing flat lettering.

背景太抢:
- clean minimal background, text-only composition, low-detail backdrop, no extra objects near strokes.

太普通:
- add explicit stroke transformation, material system, negative-space idea, connected silhouette, or themed decorations.

太花:
- reduce particles, reduce color count, remove small props, keep one material, use simple background.

不够像中文:
- emphasize authentic Chinese character structure, exact text, no pseudo-Chinese glyphs, no abstract symbols replacing radicals.

错字:
- repeat exact Chinese text early and late in the image brief; request no misspelled Chinese characters and no extra text.

## Strength Modifiers

一点 / 稍微:
- keep existing direction, adjust gently.

很 / 强烈 / 超:
- make the style dominant, but do not sacrifice legibility.

不要:
- convert into explicit negative constraint.

参考 / 类似:
- borrow mood, composition, or material; do not copy copyrighted logos or exact designs.
