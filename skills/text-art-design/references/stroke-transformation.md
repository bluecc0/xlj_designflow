# Chinese Stroke Transformation

Chinese artistic lettering must preserve character identity. Transform strokes, not the text content.

## Structure Priorities

Protect these before adding effects:

1. Correct character.
2. Recognizable radical positions.
3. Clear stroke direction.
4. Balanced inner spaces.
5. Readable silhouette at small size.

## General Character Controls

- Large character face: stronger poster impact.
- Tight center: tense, tech, action, horror.
- Open center: warm, elegant, literary, airy.
- Unified outer contour: logo-like and commercial.
- Irregular baseline: handwritten, playful, organic.
- Compressed width: cyber, film title, modern fashion.
- Expanded width: grand, ceremonial, calm.

## Stroke Transformations

Horizontal strokes:
- light bars, ribbons, clouds, water bands, metal beams, brush sweeps, paper strips.

Vertical strokes:
- pillars, light columns, bamboo sections, metal supports, falling ink, signboard strokes.

Diagonal strokes:
- motion trails, flames, wings, leaf shapes, blades, ribbons, comet tails.

Dots:
- stars, sparks, water drops, seal marks, small flowers, candy dots, light nodes.

Hooks:
- claws, waves, vine curls, metal hooks, brush hooks, cable bends.

Enclosures:
- windows, frames, shields, badges, moon gates, screens, portals.

Mouth/ring structures:
- glowing windows, stamps, candy holes, paper-cut openings, negative-space icons.

## Decoration Rules

- Decorations should attach to stroke logic, not float randomly.
- Small symbols can decorate stroke ends, holes, or negative spaces.
- Do not replace a radical with an unrelated object if it makes the character unreadable.
- Keep one dominant transformation idea per image brief unless the user wants maximalism.

## Material by Stroke Behavior

Soft styles:
- rounded terminals, inflated strokes, low contrast, pastel or warm material.

Sharp styles:
- angled cuts, high contrast, rim light, controlled fragmentation.

Organic styles:
- uneven edges, natural thickness change, ink bleed, leaf/vine/water movement.

Premium styles:
- clean silhouette, restrained bevel, subtle texture, limited palette.

Handmade styles:
- paper edges, crayon grain, marker overlap, brush irregularity, slight asymmetry.

## Brief Phrases

Use phrases like:

- "while preserving the original Chinese character structure"
- "the decorations must not cover or replace the Chinese strokes"
- "front-facing readable Chinese lettering"
- "clear silhouette and accurate radicals"
- "designed lettering, not ordinary typed font"
